Universal Hitting & Pitching Principles Across Counts

1 Never Concede Pitch One

The first pitch is a hitter’s count.

First-pitch batting average (.334) rewards aggression. A fastball down the middle is a chance to set the tone and force the pitcher to throw from behind.

2 The 1-1 Inflection Point

The fork in the road.

A 1-1 count is all about decisive contact. Winning the pitch pushes hitters to 2-1; losing it drops them into a 1-2 defensive posture.

3 Two-Strike Survival

Choke up, shorten the swing, spoil.

Swinging for power at 0-2 or 1-2 is a trap. The winning play is to shorten the zone, foul off tough pitches, and put the ball in play.
